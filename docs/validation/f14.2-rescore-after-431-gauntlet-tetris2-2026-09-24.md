## The Tetris 2 capture

The painted key is masked by captured screen `backgrounds/screen002.png`.
Three artifacts say so:

- The evaluator's build printed `sheets/unsorted.png: 1 painted tile key(s)
  are also drawn by the captured screen(s) backgrounds/screen002.png`. Its
  log flags this as defect 3, "risk, unverified".
- The scorer's render log (`score/Tetris_2__1993___Nintendo_/mesen-home/mesen.log`)
  prints `a <background> is drawn over <tile> rules: [screen002_…]<background>backgrounds/screen002.png,1,0,0,20`.
  It is at priority 20 (ADR-0050, ADR-0156).
- The sandbox index says the pack's captured screens repaint 18 454 of the
  frame's 983 040 pixels.

The earlier session reported that Tetris 2 passed after one capture was
removed. That fits the guide's *Retiring a capture* step (#344), which the
#419–#421 re-score used on Bubble Bobble, Pac-Man and The Flintstones.
**The artifacts do not record that pass.**

- All four captures (`screen001`–`screen004`) and their `<background>` lines
  are still present in the painted work copy and the baseline.
- No second score file, screenshot or render log exists.
- The only trace is the Tetris 2 pack folder's mtime. It is 22:47:26, one
  minute after the scored render (22:46:28). That is consistent with a later
  `mep/` install and cleanup, but it is not evidence of the outcome.

Also unrecoverable: which capture was removed (`screen002` is the only one
the artifacts implicate), how it was removed, and the magenta count after.

## What this does NOT prove

- **Anything about `main` after `351ee096`.** PRs #449–#488 are not in this
  binary, and #470–#488 in particular came later. That includes #472
  (untouched keeps recorded), #474 (flip shape), #479 (shape zero cell) and
  #467 (ADR-0232 CHR bank id). All of them touch the copy/record path.
- **A Tetris 2 pass.** It is reported by the earlier session and absent from
  every artifact. On disk, Tetris 2 is a criterion-3 failure caused by
  `screen002`. It is not a #431 failure.
- **That the copy tables came from a `351ee096` binary.** They were made in
  the deleted worktree, at an unknown SHA. The absence of `0F0F0F0F` shows
  #431's symptom is gone on these two frames. It does not show which build
  removed it.
- **Criteria 1, 4, 7 and 8 under a working render.** Both evaluators stopped
  before rendering. The render and score were the dispatcher's. Criterion 1
  is void (contaminated) on both runs.
- **More than one run per game.** There was one evaluator per game, and the
  other 26 F14.2 rows were not re-run.
- **Permanence.** The artifacts (`/Users/bihaiko/retest-gt2-work/runs/retest-gt2/`,
  `/Users/bihaiko/retest-gt2-sandbox/`) are not versioned.
