## New bugs

Both were confirmed with spikes, as the task required, and filed rather than
fixed.

### #431 (P1): the copy substitutes a recorded fade palette the frame never draws

`NesPackTilePalette.Resolve` returns `Substituted` (#342) when the pack keys a
tile under exactly one other palette. A bootstrap recording keys most tiles
under the all-black fade palette it saw while the screen faded in. The
Tetris 2 baseline has 474 of its 518 `<tile>` rules under `0F0F0F0F`. The
copy therefore hands out that palette for a frame drawn in `0F281807`, and
the key can never match at run time. `mep_add_cell.py`, build and lint are
all green.

Spike on Tetris 2:

- The copy table has all 960 lines on `0F0F0F0F`.
- The same state scanned with no pack gives `0F281807` (552 cells),
  `0F262320` and `0F262A12`.
- The evaluator's painted cell renders 0 magenta, even with
  `textures/backgrounds/` moved aside.
- The same cell with only its palette changed to `0F281807` renders
  274 432 magenta pixels.

Gauntlet shows the same thing: 1 023 of 1 023 lines copy as `0F0F0F0F`, while
the live palettes are `00162107` and `00180927`.

The Legend of Zelda is the same mechanism with a non-black palette. Its border
copies as `36081A28`, the title screen's palette, while this frame draws it
on `0F`. The tilemap backs every such cell in dark red, and the only cells
not backed in red were glyphs and blanks.

Before #420, the scan checked palettes against the bootstrap `auto/` pack.
That pack's `defaultTile=Y` wildcards answered `LiveMatches` for every tile,
which hid this bug.

### #432 (P1): `UI.HeadlessTests` runs native-core tests in parallel

`CopyAfterStateLoadTests` (a plain `[Fact]`, added with #419) and the
dispatcher scan (an `[AvaloniaFact]`) drive the single native core at the
same time. The project has no xUnit collection or serialization setting.
`f122_prepare_evaluator.py` runs the scan through the full
`make headless-ui-tests`, so the race reaches the evaluator's hand-over.

Out of about 35 full-suite runs:

- 12 crashed the test host with exit 134 or 139 and wrote no table.
- At least 4 wrote a table for a different frame, with no error. On
  The Legend of Zelda, the raced table held 130 title-screen keys for a
  story-scroll state: 759 of 990 positions differ from the real frame.

More than 15 runs of the scan alone (`--filter`) never crashed, and each
matched its frame. `PlayerHomeCardsTests.Continue_card_is_on_screen_once_a_game_was_played`
also failed in every full-suite log that was read.

## Protocol and deviations

The protocol is F14.2's (ADR-0214). For each game:

1. Record 60 s with `headless_record <rom> 60 <out>/rec bootstrap log`.
2. Copy `auto/` to the baseline pack and run `mep_build.py build` on it.
3. Run `scripts/f122_prepare_evaluator.py`. It chooses the frame, mints the
   `.mss`, runs the scan, draws the tilemap and makes the work copy.
4. Dispatch a fresh Opus evaluator on the unchanged briefing.
5. Score with `scripts/f122_score_panel.py` against the unpainted baseline.

F14.2's frame fallback was kept. When `choose_frame` finds no candidate
second, it hands over 40 s, or 20 s for Tetris and The Flintstones.

Deviations:

1. **The packs were re-recorded** on the new binary, so the chosen frames
   differ from F14.2's (10 s to 60 s).
2. **The guides and tools were refreshed from current `main`** in a new
   sandbox (`~/f14.2r-opus-sandbox`). `remastering-a-game.md` and
   `mep_build.py` differ from the F14.2 copies. The tool closure now
   includes `mep_capture_scan`.
3. **Prepare was retried and every table was verified, because of #432.**
   - A prepare whose scan wrote no table was re-run, up to 5 times: Dr. Mario
     took 2 attempts, Zelda II 4 and Pac-Man 1 extra.
   - Every handed-over table was compared with an isolated `--filter` scan of
     the same state and pack. Eleven matched line for line.
   - The Legend of Zelda's first table did not match. That first dispatch
     painted a title-screen key and rendered 0 magenta. It is voided as a
     harness failure (#432), not scored as a cold-read failure.
   - Prepare was re-run until the Zelda table matched (clean on the second
     loop: 30 s frame, 990 lines), and a fresh evaluator was dispatched.
4. **A false fallback was caught and redone.** The evaluator had left `mep/`
   in Zelda's pack folder, so `choose_frame` refused on a Zelda re-prepare.
   The wrapper first mistook that refusal for "no candidate second". It was
   narrowed to that exact message, and the prepare was redone.

## Observations from the passing runs

- **Dr. Mario:** the evaluator decided `mep_add_cell.py` was absent and
  placed the cell by hand. The briefing lists three tools, and this one was
  in the sandbox. It pasted the copied text unedited, so criterion 3 holds.
- **Ninja Gaiden:** the tilemap dims the boulder rows. The evaluator picked
  grass instead.
- **Super Mario Bros.:** the one-moment window reads `-8,0`, which is unusual.
- Evaluators left `mep/` installed in the pack folders. The dispatcher
  cleaned up before re-preparing.
- Elapsed time was 1 to 2.2 minutes per run.
