# ADR-0233 option A: a capture's gate is tested at every fine scroll (2026-09-25)

Validation record for the slice that implements
[ADR-0233](../adr/0233-a-captures-gate-must-be-decided-for-the-fine-scrolls-its-probes-were-never-tested-at.md)
option A ("the rival universe stops at `FineX`" — it no longer does). Owner's
pick, verbatim: *"A sozinha, mede, depois decide B (Recommended)"*. Build
go-ahead, verbatim: *"pode implementar a opção A com o DeepSeek"*.

Issue [#499](https://github.com/sbihaiko/MesenAI/issues/499) — Ninja Gaiden's
HUD frozen at 31 s by a captured `<background>` — is the bug this class is
about. It is **not closed by this slice**; the numbers are below.

## What changed

- `MesenSheets::SelectScreenAnchors` (`Core/NES/HdPacks/ScreenStitcher.cpp`):
  a frame at another `FineX` is a rival of every capture, not a skipped frame.
  It never becomes a variant — it skips `IsScreenVariant` and `AddsContent`,
  which are same-fine-scroll questions.
- `RivalSatisfiesProbe` / `RivalProbeCol`: a probe on such a rival is read at
  the cell covering the probe's absolute pixel, `x = Col*8 + captured.FineX` —
  column `(x - rival.FineX) div 8` of the rival's grid, and "no match" when
  that column is outside the grid. `PaletteMayMatch` takes the two columns
  separately, since the two frames no longer share an indexing.
- Unchanged, deliberately: `IsScreenVariant`, `AddsContent`, ADR-0159 §1's
  stability filter (variants stay same-`FineX`), and ADR-0223's emptiness-probe
  pass, which inherits the widened rival set through `GreedyAnchors`.

## Unit tests

`scripts/core_unit_tests.cpp`, five cases, all in the `BlocoP6` group:

- `TestAnchorAnotherFineScrollIsARivalNotAVariant` — the #499 shape: a capture
  at fine 0 and a byte-identical copy of it at fine 7. The copy is reported as
  a rival (1) instead of being skipped, and the screen still gets its three
  conditions.
- `TestAnchorFineScrollRivalIsReadAtTheCoveringCell` — the remap in both
  directions: the fine-7 twin differs from the capture on exactly the cell
  covering pixel 160 (its column 19, `(160-7) div 8`), and the greedy picks
  the probe that reads there.
- `TestAnchorFineScrollRemapAtTheGridEdges` — a probe left of the rival's
  first column (`(0-7) div 8 < 0`) is "no match" and separates the rival; at
  the capture's finer scroll the same probe lands in the rival's column 0 and
  is read normally.
- `TestAnchorSameFineScrollVariantsAreUntouched` — the cell a same-`FineX`
  variant changes is still kept out of the pool, with and without a rival at
  another fine scroll in the stream.
- `TestAnchorFineScrollFrameIsNeverAnAdditionRival` — a fine-7 frame that
  redraws the capture's flat block is a rival by the fine scroll, never
  counted as an addition (control: at the capture's own fine scroll it is
  counted), and a forced rival (ADR-0217 option C) takes the same remap.

RED on HEAD + tests: 1200/1204 cases pass, 4 `BlocoP6` failures, exit 2.
GREEN: 1204/1204, exit 0. Mutation (the remap reverted to "rival column =
capture column"): 1201/1204, the three remap assertions fail, then restored.
Raw output in the git-ignored `runs/0233/{red,green,mutation}.txt`.

widened universe bought nothing here — what changed is which same-`FineX`
candidates the greedy lands on (221 recorded frames matched against 180). The
raw tables and the probe sets are in the git-ignored `runs/0233/RESULTS.md`.
