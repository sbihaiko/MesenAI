# F12.13 — a variant may not add content the capture lacks (2026-09-22)

Scope and stop rule live in the PRD (Part A, row F12.13); the decision is
ADR-0221, accepted 2026-09-22 as **option B**. This log is the evidence for the
row's three stop conditions, and for the one thing it found that the row did
not anticipate: **option B, implemented exactly as written, fires on the frames
it names and does not change a single gate**, because the cells that would
separate an addition-rival from its capture are the very cells ADR-0050 forbids
as anchors. The stop condition (1) — 0 erased cells on the Punch-Out!! sweep —
is therefore **not met**: it reads 437, exactly as before. The rule was not
loosened to chase it. What it would take is measured at the end.

## What shipped

- `Core/NES/HdPacks/TileSheetTypes.h` — `IsFlatTileData`: "empty" for the kind
  test is a cell whose 16 CHR bytes resolve every pixel to one colour index
  (each plane's eight rows all `0x00` or all `0xFF`), the same "single flat
  colour per 8x8 cell" `scripts/measure_capture_overdraw.py` scores. A
  `kEmptyCell` (nothing drawn) is empty too. The recorder hands *every* drawn
  tile a shape id, so a flat tile is a shape in the grid, not `kEmptyCell` —
  which is why the definition has to look at the bytes.
- `Core/NES/HdPacks/ScreenStitcher.{h,cpp}` — `FlatShapePlane` (one flag per
  shape id) and, inside `SelectScreenAnchors`, `AddsContent`: after a frame
  clears `kAnchorVariantAgree`, it stays a variant only when every cell it
  changes is non-empty in the captured frame; one cell empty on the capture
  side and non-empty on the frame side files it as a rival.
  `AnchorChoice::AdditionRivals` counts those. `kAnchorVariantAgree` is
  untouched and the kind test runs after the ratio test, so nothing that was a
  rival became a variant.
- `Core/NES/HdPacks/HdPackBuilder.cpp` — `FinalizeScreenAnchors` builds the
  plane from `_shapeTiles` once per save and passes it; the anchor summary log
  line gains a sixth counter, `N frame(s) filed as rivals for adding content
  the capture lacks`. Net zero lines (the file sits on its ADR-0137 ceiling,
  2428).
- `scripts/core_unit_tests.cpp` — six cases on a synthetic `GridFrame` pair:
  addition → rival (with the pre-change control showing the #339 gate),
  content-for-content → variant, below-threshold → rival with the kind test
  never counting it, `kEmptyCell` counts as empty, a frame that *blanks* a
  cell stays a variant, and `IsFlatTileData`/`FlatShapePlane` against the
  harness's definition. `make core-unit-tests`: **927/927** (914 before).
- `scripts/measure_capture_draw_rate.py` (+ `test_measure_capture_draw_rate.py`)
  — the per-capture draw rate stop condition (3) asks for, read off the
  recording's grid dump with no re-render: a gate fires on a played frame when
  every probe finds its tile and palette at its cell; CHR ROM probes resolve
  the index through the ROM's own CHR block.

Nothing in `CaptureScreen`, the numbering, ADR-0156 residency or the render
path changed.

### Why, read off the grid dump

`screen003`'s gate fires on 80 consecutive retained frames (drawn-frame
14.0–23.3 s; the sweep's 18–27 s wall clock, the difference being the blank
frames at power-on). Against the first of them, each later frame differs on
one more cell than the one before — 1, 2, 3 … 79 — and the diff sets are
**nested**: the game types the card one letter every four frames, starting at
cell (col 12, row 13). Every one of those cells is shape 97 in the capture:
`00000000000000000000000000000000`, the flat backdrop. So:

- each frame *is* an addition under option B (the after log's 228 covers this
  card and the fight screens), and is now a rival;
- to separate the capture from the frame with one letter, the gate must probe
  **the cell the letter lands on** — there is no other cell that differs;
- that cell is flat on the capture side. ADR-0050 chooses anchors among the
  "rarest **non-flat** tiles on screen", and `HdPackBuilder::CaptureScreen`
  drops every flat run before ranking (`isFlat(run.Tile) || …` → `continue`),
  so the cell never reaches `pending.Cells`, never reaches `SelectScreenAnchors`,
  and no fallback inside the stitcher can reach for it.

This is structural, not a threshold: **the capture side of an addition is
empty by definition, and an empty cell is exactly what ADR-0050 excludes.** An
addition-only rival can never be separated under ADR-0050's candidate rule,
whatever ADR-0221 B says about its kind. ADR-0221's sentence "*which it can,
since 41 cells differ*" assumed the differing cells were usable as probes; on
this card, none is.

The **41–43 s occurrence** is the same mechanism on a different capture: it
falls in the window where `screen009` and `screen010` both fire (drawn-frame
36.2–39.4 s), the fight screen filling in after its capture.

## What it would take — measured, not shipped

To check that the diagnosis above is the whole story — and to put a number on
what the row's stop condition actually costs — a **prototype** was built in a
scratch copy of this tree (never in the worktree, not in the diff, not
proposed here): `CaptureScreen` keeps the flat runs as candidates too, expanded
to every cell they cover and marked `Usage = UINT32_MAX` so the ADR-0050
rarity pools ignore them; `SelectScreenAnchors`, only when rivals survive the
stable and wide passes, runs one more greedy pass over the stable pool plus
the flat cells no variant changes ("emptiness probes") and keeps that result
only if it separates more rivals. Same route, same 36 renders.

```
10 screen(s) anchored (4 on a cell a variant may change, 6 still matching another
recorded screen, 1 skipped for an earlier capture's gate, 0 dropped post-hoc,
228 frame(s) filed as rivals for adding content the capture lacks)
```

`screen003`–`screen006` each gain an emptiness probe on the card — tile `FD`
(the flat backdrop) at rows 120, 136, 176, 200 — next to the two title cells
they shared before:

| | frames losing content | erased cells | 19–27 s | 41–43 s | 44–45 s | captures | mean draw rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| after (option B, shipped) | 12 of 36 | 437 | 10 → 71 | 18, 18, 27 | 0, 0 | 10 | 0.1200 |
| prototype (B + emptiness probes) | 5 of 36 | **141** | **all 0** | 18, 18, 27 | 34, 44 | 9 | 0.0865 |

So the card is exactly what the diagnosis says: once a probe may sit on an
empty cell, the 79 nested addition-rivals are all separated and 19–27 s read
zero. The **fight screen is not**: 41–43 s stay 18/18/27, and 44–45 s pick up
34 and 44 because the prototype's `screen009` (the one capture left of the two
fight screens; `screen010` was skipped as sharing an earlier capture's gate)
now fires across the whole window with a different gate
(`201,136,01FE / 169,168,01FF / 9,16,9C` against the shipped
`105,72,E5 / 9,16,9C / 73,16,A1`) that still does not separate the frames
where the fight fills in. Why the fight screen resists where the card yields
was **not traced** in this log — the grid dump for that window is in the
scratch recording and is the next thing to read. It is a different problem
from the card, and the flat-cell candidate rule alone does not close it.

Two things the prototype makes concrete for whoever amends ADR-0050/0159:

1. the candidate clause must admit flat cells, or addition-rivals stay
   unseparable by construction — the kind test alone changes nothing;
2. even then the fight screens keep erasing; 0 erased cells on this route
   also needs a way to separate frames that differ only under sprites.

Neither is in this slice. The prototype's numbers are here so the next
decision starts from 141 and the two residual windows, not from 437.
