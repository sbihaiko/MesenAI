## 1. Which golden games have no committed route past their first stall

The golden list is the 21 folders under `scripts/stages/`. A map is wanted
where position, camera, room and HP describe progress, and where the only
committed route does not get past the game's first stall.

## 2. The two maps

Each field was first taken from a public RAM map, which was treated as a
hypothesis only. A field was kept only when a probe through
`scripts/step_emu.py`, reading the full 2 KB every 10–20 frames under idle,
Right and Left, showed it behaving as named on the pinned dump. The
`verification` block in each map is the two-checkpoint experiment
`scripts/test_ram_maps.py` replays.

**Castlevania**: `scripts/stages/castlevania/ram-map.json`. Pinned dump: No-Intro
`3DCB69A8…332EDA3A`, whole file `7A20C44F…CAE7B546`. Start state:
`mint-stage1.txt` at 6 s.

| Checkpoint (input) | lives `$002A` | hp `$0045` | room `$0028` | camera `$002E/F` | abs x `$0040/1` | screen x | progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| courtyard (400f -) | 4 | 64 | 0 | 0 | 50 | 50 | 50 |
| walked (+480f R) | 4 | 64 | 0 | 402 | 530 | 128 | 530 |
| hall (+400f R) | 4 | 64 | **1** | 0 | 108 | 108 | 4204 |
| hit (+300f R) | 4 | **56** | 1 | 190 | 318 | 128 | 4414 |
| respawned (+2000f R) | **3** | 56 | 1 | 0 | 121 | 121 | 4217 |

- The area index `$0028` is the `room`. It changes 0 → 1 at the castle door,
  and both camera and abs x restart there.
- `progress` is therefore `room * 4096 + abs_x`, which stays monotone across
  the door. The hall's camera stops at 1280, so one area is narrower than
  4096.
- A zombie hit costs 8 HP.

**Mega Man 2**: `scripts/stages/megaman2/ram-map.json`. Pinned dump: No-Intro

Plain Right walks through the gate, at abs x about 640. The search's jump
macros carry Simon past that point to the wall, and it stops there. This is
the kind of stall ADR-0242's recorder exists for.
